Frequently Asked Questions

PNG supports transparency and uses lossless compression (better quality, larger files). JPG uses lossy compression (smaller files, slight quality loss). Choose PNG when you need transparency or maximum quality.

PNG images will preserve transparency if the original PDF has transparent elements. Pages with white backgrounds will show as white in the PNG output.

PNG images are generated at 300 DPI by default, providing high-quality output suitable for printing and detailed viewing.
