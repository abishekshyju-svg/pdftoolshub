Frequently Asked Questions

Scanned PDFs contain images rather than text. Basic OCR may extract some content, but results vary. For best results, use PDFs with selectable text.

Plain text does not support formatting. You get the raw text content without bold, italic, or font styles. For formatted output, use our PDF to Word converter.

Text is output in UTF-8 encoding, which supports all languages and special characters found in your PDF document.
