Frequently Asked Questions

With low and medium compression, quality loss is minimal and usually unnoticeable. High compression may slightly reduce image quality but significantly decreases file size.

File size reduction depends on the content. Image-heavy PDFs can typically be reduced by 50-80%. Text-only PDFs may see smaller reductions of 10-30%.
