Frequently Asked Questions

Our converter does its best to maintain the original layout, fonts, tables, and images. Complex layouts with many columns or decorative elements may need minor adjustments.

Scanned PDFs (image-based) require OCR technology to extract text. Basic text extraction is supported, but complex scanned documents may have limited accuracy.

The output file is in DOCX format, which is compatible with Microsoft Word 2007 and later, Google Docs, LibreOffice, and other modern word processors.
